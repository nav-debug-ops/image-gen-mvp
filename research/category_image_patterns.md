# Main Image Pattern Summary: All 20 Amazon Categories

This document summarizes the dominant main image patterns observed across 20 Amazon product categories, covering both Top-performing (high BSR) and High-CTR product image strategies.

---

## Category 1: Electronics

- Clean white backgrounds dominate (90%+ of top sellers)
- Product-only shots with no lifestyle elements or props
- High-CTR images show the product at 45-degree angles with slight shadows for depth
- Scale fills 80-90% of frame - products appear large and premium
- Multiple components shown together (earbuds + case, cables included)
- High contrast with deep blacks and bright product colors
- Metallic finishes and glossy surfaces are highlighted with strategic lighting
- Top-performing products show straightforward front-facing or slight angle views; High-CTR adds dynamic perspectives

---

## Category 2: Home & Kitchen

- White backgrounds dominant but lifestyle settings more common than electronics (20-30%)
- Product scale varies - smaller items fill frame, larger appliances shown in context
- Color is key differentiator - vibrant product colors (teal, pink, rose gold) used strategically
- Many show product 'in use' state: filled water bottles, deployed cleaning tools, plated food
- High-CTR images often include subtle props (water droplets, food items, plants) for context
- Texture emphasis - stainless steel, fabric, silicone are showcased through lighting
- Top-performing products favor clean product-only shots; High-CTR adds lifestyle elements
- Kitchen appliances show open/closed states or multiple angles in single image

---

## Category 3: Beauty & Personal Care

- White backgrounds universal, but product packaging color is the primary differentiator
- Skincare often shows product with pump/dropper cap removed to display texture
- K-beauty products use vibrant packaging (pink, blue, purple) for shelf appeal
- Many show before/after skin closeups or model faces (but NOT in main image per Amazon TOS)
- Product positioning: straight-on for tubes/bottles, slight angle for jars to show depth
- High-CTR images include product texture swatches, applicators shown separately, or droplets
- Minimalist aesthetic dominates - clean, clinical, dermatologist-approved vibe
- Top-performing stick to product-only; High-CTR add texture demos or product groupings

---

## Category 4: Sports & Outdoors

- Mix of white backgrounds (60%) and lifestyle/outdoor settings (40%)
- Action shots more common - products shown in-use or ready-to-use state
- Bright, bold product colors emphasized (neon yellow, bright orange, electric blue)
- Equipment shown at dynamic angles suggesting motion or athleticism
- Scale communicated through human hands, bodies, or familiar objects
- Outdoor/nature backgrounds used for camping, hiking, water bottles
- High-CTR images show products in action or demonstrate key features visually
- Top-performing favor clean product shots; High-CTR embrace lifestyle contexts

---

## Category 5: Toys & Games

- White backgrounds standard (80%+) to show toy colors clearly
- Toys shown fully assembled or in play-ready state, not in packaging
- Bright primary colors emphasized - reds, blues, yellows dominate
- Multiple components or pieces shown together demonstrates play value
- Scale important - toys shown with hands or age-appropriate context
- LEGO and building toys show completed builds plus loose bricks for creative appeal
- High-CTR images show toys in action or demonstrate key play features
- Licensed character toys feature characters prominently for brand recognition

---

## Category 6: Clothing & Apparel

- Pure white backgrounds mandatory per Amazon policy; all top sellers comply
- Apparel shown either flat-lay or on-model (ghost mannequin common for structured garments)
- Single garment focus; multi-packs shown stacked or fanned to indicate quantity
- No visible packaging — product only, tags sometimes visible for authenticity
- Front-facing or slight 3/4 angle; even diffused lighting with no harsh shadows
- High-CTR images show fabric texture clearly with tight crop filling 85%+ of frame
- Color accuracy is critical — top sellers show true-to-life color with calibrated white balance
- High-CTR set uses ghost mannequin for 3D shape visualization vs flat-lay in top-performing set

---

## Category 7: Food & Grocery

- Packaging-forward images dominate — most food items show the actual retail packaging/container
- Clean white backgrounds standard; product centered and filling 70-85% of frame
- Bundles and multi-packs common (variety packs, 12-packs, bulk bags)
- For beverages, cans/bottles shown at slight angle with visible branding
- Bright, saturated colors on packaging create visual pop at thumbnail size
- Fresh food items (fruits, meats) shown unpackaged with studio lighting
- High-CTR images show packaging with flavor/variety clearly readable at small sizes
- Top-performing images rely on brand recognition; High-CTR images add visual interest through packaging arrangement

---

## Category 8: Pet Supplies

- Pure white backgrounds with product-only focus (no pets in main images typically)
- Packaging shown for consumables (treats, food); product-only for accessories
- Multi-packs and bulk quantities commonly displayed in organized arrangements
- Products fill 75-90% of frame with tight cropping
- Even lighting with soft shadows; bright colors on packaging pop against white
- High-CTR images show product scale clearly (bags with size indicators, count visible)
- Top-performing set relies on brand trust; High-CTR set uses visual cues for value (quantity, size)
- Treat/food categories show packaging front-facing; accessories shown at use-angle

---

## Category 9: Health & Household

- Product packaging front-facing on pure white background is the universal standard
- Household essentials show retail packaging prominently (paper towels, tissues, batteries)
- Health supplements show bottle/container with label clearly readable
- Multi-pack quantities shown stacked or arranged to emphasize value (12-pack, 30-roll)
- Product fills 70-85% of frame; tight crop but not claustrophobic
- Even soft lighting; no harsh shadows; packaging colors must be true-to-retail
- High-CTR images use bold packaging colors (yellow, blue, green) that pop at thumbnail
- Top-performing relies on brand familiarity; High-CTR creates visual impact through packaging design

---

## Category 10: Baby Products

- Packaging-forward for consumables (diapers, wipes); product-only for gear (monitors, seats)
- Clean white backgrounds universal; pastel colors on packaging signal baby category
- Multi-pack displays common — wipe refill packs, diaper bundles shown in organized stacks
- Product fills 75-85% of frame; soft, warm lighting tone throughout
- Baby gear shows product at 3/4 angle with all features visible in single shot
- Trust signals visible: safety certifications, hypoallergenic labels on packaging
- High-CTR images show clear count/quantity on packaging readable at thumbnail
- Top-performing leverages brand trust (Pampers, Huggies); High-CTR uses clean design and visible value cues

---

## Category 11: Automotive

- Clean white or light gray backgrounds dominate, with most products shown against pure white
- Products typically fill 60-80% of frame, centered with balanced cropping
- Single product focus is standard; bundles show organized arrangement with clear hierarchy
- Minimal or no packaging visible - product-only presentation preferred
- Straight-on or 45-degree angles with soft, even studio lighting eliminating harsh shadows
- High-CTR images feature bold colors (blue, red, black) and clear size indicators or scale references
- Top-performing images emphasize functionality with subtle lifestyle context (dashboard/car interior hints)
- Product features are often highlighted through transparent overlays or callout graphics showing key specs

---

## Category 12: Tools & Home Improvement

- Pure white backgrounds are universal standard, creating maximum product visibility
- Products occupy 70-85% of frame with tools often shown at slight angles to display features
- Single tool focus dominates; combo kits show organized layout with primary tool prominent
- Packaging rarely shown except for consumables (filters, paper) where it indicates quantity
- Three-quarter view angles common for power tools to show controls, batteries, and ergonomics
- High-CTR images emphasize bold brand colors (DeWalt yellow, Milwaukee red) and visible specifications
- Top-performing images balance technical detail with clean simplicity
- Accessories included often laid out in grid pattern to show value and completeness

---

## Category 13: Office Products

- Clean white backgrounds universal, with some planners/calendars using lifestyle staging
- Products occupy 60-75% of frame; paper products often show ream packaging, planners shown open
- Single product standard; multi-packs show organized stacking or fanned arrangement
- Packaging prominent for paper/supplies (conveys quantity), minimal for stationery
- Flat lay or slight elevation angles for most products; markers/pens shown at 45 degrees
- High-CTR images use vibrant product colors (planner covers, marker sets) or lifestyle contexts
- Top-performing images emphasize clarity, brand recognition, and value (sheet count, pack size)
- Planners often show sample pages with visible organization features and monthly/weekly layouts

---

## Category 14: Garden & Outdoor

- White backgrounds standard, though some lifestyle shots show products in garden settings
- Products fill 65-80% of frame; solar lights often shown as multiples to indicate pack quantity
- Single item or matched sets common; seed packs show front packaging with variety details
- Packaging emphasized for seeds (variety count, organic certification badges) and gloves
- Straight-on views for tools/gloves, slight elevation for hoses and lights, flat for seed packs
- High-CTR images feature bright colors (solar light glow, vibrant seed packet graphics, colored gloves)
- Top-performing images balance product clarity with use-case context (hose with nozzle attached)
- Seed packets often show photo of mature plant/vegetable alongside packet for expectation setting

---

## Category 15: Arts, Crafts & Sewing

- White backgrounds universal, with craft products often shown with sample project results
- Products fill 70-85% of frame; marker sets fanned to show colors, bead kits show compartments open
- Single product or complete kit display; paint markers arranged by color gradient for visual appeal
- Minimal packaging except for value packs where box/case communicates quantity
- Flat lay dominant for markers/supplies, slight angle for 3D kits showing contents
- High-CTR images maximize color impact (rainbow marker arrays, colorful beads) and show creative potential
- Top-performing images balance color variety display with clear product functionality
- DIY kits often show both packaged product AND finished project examples to demonstrate value

---

## Category 16: Industrial & Scientific

- Pure white or light gray backgrounds dominate; industrial products favor high-contrast imaging
- Product fills 70-85% of frame; larger items (label makers, safety equipment) shown at slight angle
- Single product focus standard; multi-packs show organized bundle with quantity clearly visible
- Packaging visible for consumables (cable ties, gloves); product-only for equipment and tools
- Straight-on or 3/4 angle views emphasizing functional design details and product features
- High-CTR images use color contrast (yellow warning colors, safety orange) to draw attention
- Top-performing images rely on brand trust and clear product functionality over lifestyle context
- Scale indicators (hand holding product, size callouts) help buyers assess dimensions quickly

---

## Category 17: Musical Instruments

- White backgrounds dominate (85%+) with instruments shown product-only against clean white
- Instruments fill 70-90% of frame; guitars shown full-length, small accessories fill tighter crop
- Single instrument standard; accessory sets (pick packs, string sets) show multi-pack arrangement
- No packaging shown for instruments; some accessories show retail card packaging
- Guitars and ukuleles shown straight-on from front; microphones at 3/4 angle to show form
- High-CTR images use product color (bold red guitars, sunburst finishes) as primary visual hook
- Top-performing images emphasize complete product with all included accessories visible
- High-CTR vs Top-performing: High-CTR uses lifestyle-adjacent color and texture; Top-performing relies on brand recognition and spec clarity

---

## Category 18: Books & Media

- White backgrounds near-universal; physical books and journals shown as product-only shots
- Books shown straight-on front cover filling 70-85% of frame; cover art is the primary visual
- Journals and planners often shown open at slight angle to reveal interior page layouts
- Coloring books shown with sample completed page visible to demonstrate art quality
- Multi-volume sets shown stacked or lined up to communicate series scope and value
- High-CTR images leverage bold cover colors (red, navy, gold, black) with high legibility titles
- Top-performing books rely on author name and title clarity; High-CTR uses cover art visual impact
- Subscription products (Audible, Kindle Unlimited) use digital mock-up screen-style imagery

---

## Category 19: Jewelry & Watches

- White backgrounds dominate but gradient and subtle colored backgrounds used more than other categories
- Jewelry shown on white bust/mannequin neck for necklaces; earrings on ear display or flat-lay
- Watches shown at slight 3/4 angle to display face, case, and band simultaneously
- High-CTR images show jewelry with visible sparkle or shimmer effects from strategic lighting
- Close-crop fills 80-90% of frame for fine jewelry; smartwatches shown with screen displaying UI
- Top-performing images show single clean product with maximum sparkle; High-CTR adds lifestyle context
- Gift-ready packaging (velvet box, ribbon) shown in thumbnail adds premium gifting signal
- Apple Watch bands and smartwatch accessories show on wrist context for scale and lifestyle appeal

---

## Category 20: Shoes & Handbags

- Pure white backgrounds mandatory; single shoe shown as pair (both shoes) or single shoe at 3/4 angle
- Shoes fill 70-85% of frame; shown from 3/4 front-facing angle that reveals toe box, profile, and sole
- Handbags shown stuffed/shaped on white background; straps and hardware details prominently displayed
- No lifestyle or model shots in main image; product-only standard across all top sellers
- Color is the primary purchase driver; top sellers show shoe/bag in most popular colorway
- High-CTR images use bold, distinctive colors (neon, candy colors for Crocs; rich tones for bags)
- Top-performing images show single pair clearly; High-CTR uses color as primary differentiator
- Texture detail visible on bags (pebbled leather, canvas) creates tactile quality signal at thumbnail size
